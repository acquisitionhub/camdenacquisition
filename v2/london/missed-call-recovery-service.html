FAQ

Common Questions About Missed Call Recovery

How does missed call recovery work for a dental practice?

The moment a call goes unanswered, our system detects it and sends a personalised SMS to the caller within seconds, including your practice name and a direct link to your online booking page. Most patients book within 20 minutes — often during their commute on the Tube.

How many calls does a typical Camden dental practice miss per week?

Based on practices we work with across NW1 and surrounding postcodes, the average is around 5 missed calls per week. During busy morning periods near transport hubs, this can be significantly higher.

Does missed call SMS recovery work after hours?

Yes. The system runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If a patient calls on a Saturday evening and can't get through, they receive an instant SMS with your booking link.

Does my reception team need to do anything differently?

No. The system runs entirely in the background. You get a missed call tracking dashboard so you can see how many calls were missed and recovered — but you don't need to manage it day to day.

Is the SMS GDPR compliant?

Yes, fully. All messaging complies with UK GDPR. We provide complete guidance on consent and data handling during onboarding. Patient data is never sold or shared with third parties.
