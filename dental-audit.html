    Questions

    Who is this for?

    Agencies and freelancers who market dental practices and want a specific reason to contact a practice. It isn't built for practices to audit themselves.

    How do the dental checks work?

    We read the practice's own website and look for treatment and patient-type terms like implants, Invisalign, NHS and emergency. It shows whether they're mentioned, not how good the pages are.

    Where does the Google data come from?

    Google's official Places API. Only public profile information, the same details anyone sees on Google Maps.

    What does "lifetime" mean?

    You pay once and keep your tier for as long as the product is offered, including improvements to your tier's features.
