Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Who we are

Camden Acquisition Hub Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is a dental marketing agency registered in England and Wales, operating from 5-7 Buck Street, London NW1 8NJ. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller, registration number ZC148168.

What data we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide via forms, email, or phone — name, practice name, postcode, email address, phone number
  • Information submitted through our free tools (e.g. the New Patient Acquisition Score)
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, referrer, general location) via standard analytics
  • On behalf of client practices, we may process patient contact details solely to deliver review-request and missed-call-recovery messaging — see "Data processed on behalf of clients" below

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and book strategy calls
  • To deliver the services a client practice has engaged us for
  • To improve our website and marketing
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

Data processed on behalf of clients

Where a dental practice engages Camden Acquisition Hub or Try Review Bloom, we act as a data processor for that practice's patient contact details, used only to send post-appointment SMS/email review requests and missed-call follow-up messages. This data is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing purposes, or used outside the scope the client practice has authorised.

Legal basis

We process personal data under UK GDPR on the basis of legitimate interest (responding to enquiries, improving our service), consent (marketing communications, patient SMS/email requests), and contract (delivering services to engaged clients).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at julien@camdenacquisitionhub.com.

Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected for, or as required by law.
