Common questions

Answered honestly.

How quickly will I see results?

Most Camden practices recover their first missed-call patient within 24–48 hours of going live. Review volume starts climbing in the first week. Local Google Maps ranking improvements for NW1 searches typically become visible within 4–8 weeks.

Do I need to change anything in my practice?

No. Everything runs in the background. No new workflows for your reception team, no software to learn. The only requirement is that your PMS or booking system can share basic appointment completion signals — we handle the rest during onboarding.

Which scope is right for my practice?

If you have fewer than 50 Google reviews, start with Scope 01 (Get Found). If you're actively competing in NW1 and want to reach the Map Pack, Scope 02 is your plan. Scope 03 is for established practices that want total postcode dominance.

Is the SMS messaging UK GDPR compliant?

Yes, fully. All patient communications comply with UK GDPR. We provide complete guidance on consent collection during onboarding. Patient data is never sold or shared with any third party.

Can I upgrade between scopes?

Yes — you can move between scopes at any time with 30 days notice. Most practices start on Scope 01, see results within the first 90 days, and move to Scope 02 when they're ready to actively compete in the Map Pack.

Do you work with NHS as well as private practices?

Yes. We work with NHS practices, private clinics, cosmetic dentists, implant specialists, and mixed practices across Camden Town, Kentish Town, Chalk Farm, Primrose Hill, and all NW1, NW3, and NW5 postcodes.
