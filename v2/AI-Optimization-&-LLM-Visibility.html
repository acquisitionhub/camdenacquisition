AI Optimization & LLM Visibility: The New Frontier of Dental Practice Marketing in 2026

While your competitors focus on Google rankings, a new wave of patients is finding their dentist through AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini — and most practices are completely invisible there.

7 min readSEO · AI SearchCamden Acquisition Hub

Something significant has shifted in how patients find dental practices in 2026. Google is still dominant — but it's no longer the only game in town. A growing number of patients are now asking AI assistants questions like "what's the best dentist for implants near me?" — and the answers those AI systems provide are increasingly influencing which practices get the call.

If your practice is invisible in large language model (LLM) search results, you are about to start losing patients to practices that have figured this out.

What Is LLM / AI Optimisation?

Traditional SEO optimises your website to rank in Google's blue links. AI Optimisation (AIO) is the practice of creating content and establishing digital presence signals that help AI assistants — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews — surface your practice when patients ask conversational questions. Most dental practices are not doing any of it.

The goal is to appear prominently when someone asks an AI assistant 'what are my options for stained teeth?' — to have your practice's information served as the primary answer.

Google Maps Domination Still Matters

While AI search is growing, Google Maps and local search remain the most direct path to high-intent patients. In 2026, a fully optimised Google Business Profile is non-negotiable: complete service listings for every procedure you offer, recent photos, consistent review generation, and active Q&A management.

Content That Works for Both AI and Humans

The good news is that the content strategy that helps you rank in AI results also helps you rank in traditional search. Create detailed, authoritative, genuinely helpful content around your core services — not keyword-stuffed pages, but real answers to real patient questions.

  • Audit your Google Business Profile for completeness and accuracy
  • Build a systematic monthly review generation process
  • Create detailed service pages answering the questions patients actually ask
  • Develop FAQ content targeting conversational, long-tail queries
  • Test your practice's visibility by asking AI tools questions patients would ask
  • Work with a digital marketing partner who understands AI search optimisation
